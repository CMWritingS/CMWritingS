About Chandra Mohan

A passionate storyteller, CHANDRA MOHAN crafts engaging tales that inspire and resonate with readers. With a unique voice and keen observation of life, his work spans genres, blending creativity and emotion.

Dedicated to making an impact, he aims to connect hearts and minds through the power of words. Every story crafted here is a journey — from tales of love and family to explorations of social issues.
